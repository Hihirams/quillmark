    01 / THE METHOD

    How scoring works

    A detector cannot prove who wrote something. Use the signals alongside the original text.

    1. {{ engines|length }} engines read the text

      Neural classifiers, statistical tests of word predictability and checks for recurring phrasing look at different signals.

    2. One calibrated score

      The votes are combined by a model fitted on SlopBench. Fewer than 2 in 100 human texts in the benchmark score {{ bands[2]|int }} or higher.

    3. Every vote shown

      The report shows each engine's score and tells you when the engines disagree.
